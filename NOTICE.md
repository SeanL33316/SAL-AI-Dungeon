## Story Arc Engine

**Project:** Story Arc Engine  
**Author:** Yi1i1i  
**Repository:** https://github.com/Yi1i1i/Story-Arc-Engine

For that reason, this SAL repository does not include a copy of Story Arc Engine's source code. The included browser builder downloads the upstream source directly from the original repository at the user's request.
